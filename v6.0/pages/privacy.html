Privacy policy.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This covers the Chrome extension Not yet. It keeps YouTube and any other sites you choose closed until your list is done, and closes them at bedtime. Here is everything it does with your information.

What it stores

Your habits, tasks, sticky notes, timer history and settings. All of it is saved in your own browser using Chrome's extension storage. It never leaves your computer, and nobody else can see it, including the developer.

What it fetches

If you connect a calendar, the extension downloads that calendar's feed directly from your calendar provider (for example Google Calendar) so it can show your events and turn tagged ones into tasks. The feed address you paste in is stored only in your browser. It asks for permission to read that one address when you connect it.

Nothing else is fetched. There are no analytics, ads, trackers, accounts or servers.

What it does on the sites it closes

On YouTube, and on any other site you switch on, the extension checks the address of the page you're on so it can show the lock screen or close the site at bedtime. On YouTube it also reads the length of the video you start on a school night. It asks Chrome for access to each extra site only when you switch that site on. It doesn't record or send your browsing or viewing history anywhere.

Deleting your data

Settings → Backup → Delete everything erases it all. Removing the extension from Chrome does the same.
