Calendar

Where do I find that?
  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer and click the gear, then Settings.
  2. Under Settings for my calendars, pick your calendar.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.

Outlook and iCloud work too: use their “publish” or “public calendar” link.
