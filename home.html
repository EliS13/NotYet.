A Chrome extension for students. Your homework, habits and calendar in one list, and YouTube stays closed until today's list is done.

Get it on the Chrome Web Store

What it does

  • One list for the day. Homework, habits, tasks and calendar events together, with how long each one takes.
  • Homework first. YouTube, and any other site you choose, shows a lock screen until today's list is done. On school nights they close at bedtime.
  • Help getting started. Timers, "just 5 minutes", breaking a task into steps, and focus sounds.
  • Tests on the way. A countdown to your next test, with short study sessions spread over the days before it.
  • Your own stats. How long each subject really takes you, and your streak.
  • Your calendars and apps. Optionally sign in with Google to see and add Google Calendar events and Google Tasks, from one or more accounts. Todoist, Canvas and calendar links work too.

Your information

Everything Not yet. saves stays in your own browser. Signing in with Google is optional, and your calendars and tasks go only between your browser and Google, never to the developer. The privacy policy has the details.
